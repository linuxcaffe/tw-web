# The menu

Click the logo at the left of the nav bar, and the menu slides out from the left.
With the keyboard: `Esc` puts the focus on the logo, `Enter` opens the menu, `↑` and `↓` move through it, and `Esc` closes it again.

[image: the side menu open, with the version and update pill under the logo]

## At the top

Under the logo are tw-web's version and, when there's one, the yellow **Update** pill: see [Updating](update.md).
Click the logo at the top again to close the menu.

## The items

**About** says what Taskwarrior and tw-web are, with links to [taskwarrior.org](https://taskwarrior.org) and the awesome-taskwarrior fleet.

**Projects** is the project tree, the same as the List's left sidebar: indented by level, a count beside each, an arrow to fold a parent away.
Click a project to filter by it.
On a phone, where the sidebars don't fit, this is how you get to them.

**Tags** is the Tags sidebar in a panel, with its three modes, **active**, **all** and **virtual** (see [List](list.md#the-sidebars)).

**Stats** is `task stats`: how many tasks of each kind, how big the data is, and more, as a table.

**Settings** is where tw-web's own settings live: see [Settings](settings.md).

**Sync** opens the Sync dialog, for [gittw](https://github.com/linuxcaffe/gittw): what's waiting to go, a **Sync Now** button, and **Undo** for the last change to a task. See [Syncing](sync.md).

**Terminal** opens the console with your own shell (or press `t`): see [The terminal console](console.md).

**Help** is this Help. `?` opens it from anywhere, and `Esc` or `?` takes you back to the view you came from.

## At the bottom

The footer names this **station** (its `gittw.device`, when gittw is set up), so on a phone and a laptop you know which you're looking at.

**nb-web** appears when you have an `nb` annotation launcher with a web address: it opens your notes (`N` does the same). See [Working with nb](nb.md).

**Stop** stops this tw-web server, after you confirm. It stays stopped until you start it again; see [Installing](install.md#starting-it).

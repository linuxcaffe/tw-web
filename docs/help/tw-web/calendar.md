# Calendar

Keys: [Keyboard shortcuts](../keys.md#calendar). Holidays, your own events and the sky: [Holidays, events, moon & seasons](layers.md).

## Zoom

Week and Day each have three steps of row size and start hour (Settings → Week/Day zoom steps).
Clicking the active view's button, or pressing its key, moves to the next step; changing view or reloading goes back to the default.

## On a phone

Press and hold a task (about a third of a second) to pick it up, then move it; the page stays put.
A quick sideways swipe goes to the next or previous week, day or month.
In Week and Day, pinch to step through the zoom steps: spread your fingers for smaller rows, pinch them together for bigger ones; the time between your fingers stays put.
